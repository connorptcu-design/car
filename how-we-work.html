How we work

Published fees, a retainer, and no stake in whether you transact.

Consulting fees in this industry are usually a negotiation you enter without information. Ours are on the website, every step stands alone, and none of them obliges you to the next.

The engagement

Agreement audit

We read your buy-sell, operating agreement and any equity documents, then give you a findings list: how price is set, whether discounts apply, what is funded and for which triggers, who can veto a buyer, and what happens on death, disability or divorce.

$3,000fixed, two weeks

Buy-in review, for the next generation

For an advisor being offered equity. What it is, what it is worth, whether it works against your real income, and what must be in the document. Confidential to you.

$2,000fixed, two weeks

Readiness diagnostic

The full picture delivered to the partner group. Structure, client transferability, restated economics, a scorecard against what a buyer's diligence team will test, and a sequenced plan. Includes interviews with the next generation conducted without the founder present.

$9,500fixed, six weeks

Preparation retainer

Doing the work rather than describing it. Relationship transition waves, restating the books, the agreement, the service model, building the bench, and the founder and successor conversations neither of them can have alone.

$3,500per month, 12 to 24 months

Transaction support

Alongside your banker and counsel, on your side of the table. Diligence preparation, reviewing structures and earnout mechanics, and modelling what each offer actually nets you.

$350per hour

Firm and team workshops

A half or full day for a broker-dealer, custodian, study group or advisor cohort. The diagnostic run live with the room's own numbers, plus the succession conversation framework for founders and successors.

from $5,000per session

The order it happens in

Two items go first regardless of how they score, because both take the longest and both gate everything downstream.

1

Find out what is actually true

The agreement, the restated economics, and who owns the client relationships according to the CRM rather than to anyone's memory. The gap between those two answers is usually the finding.

Weeks one to six
2

Start moving relationships, immediately

Before anything else is fixed, because it is the only item measured in years. Structured joint meetings on the largest households, with someone other than the founder leading the agenda.

Month two onward, continuously
3

Make the business legible

Accrual books, a documented add-back schedule, a written service model the CRM can prove was delivered, and the agreement rewritten to match what the firm has become.

Months three to eighteen
4

Decide the path, then run it

Internal, external or platform, chosen on what each nets after tax rather than on headline price. Then diligence preparation alongside your banker and counsel.

Month eighteen onward

What we do not do

We do not appraise practices for regulatory or tax purposes, broker transactions, sell securities, manage money, or accept compensation from buyers, lenders or platforms. When you need a formal valuation, an intermediary, counsel or a tax advisor, we will tell you and help you choose one.

Why there is no success fee Valuation firms and sell-side bankers are paid when a transaction closes. That is an honest model, and it means none of them can tell a founder to wait three years, which is the correct advice a good share of the time. A retainer can.
