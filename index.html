What actually makes this different

Every other firm in this business earns on transactions. We do not. We take a retainer for preparation work and an hourly rate if you want us alongside you in a transaction, never a success fee, and never a referral fee from a buyer, a lender or a platform. That means the advice to wait three years is always available to us.

The national firms serve independent RIAs well. Advisors inside wirehouses, insurance career systems and broker-dealers face a different question, because their book may not be freely salable at all, and that group is poorly served today.

We also work in person, in Texas, Louisiana and Arkansas. The national firms are remote.

Two very different conversations

Succession involves two people who want different things from the same transition: the advisor who built the practice, and the advisor expected to carry it forward. We work with both, usually one at a time before bringing them together.

You built the practice

You are somewhere between 3 and 10 years from wanting to exit. Unless you have an exit plan, that deadline may get pushed out further than you expect.

  • What your practice is worth today, and what is driving that number
  • What the same practice could be worth once it is prepared for a sale
  • Whether a buyer, inside your firm or outside it, could get financing at the price you have in mind
  • Whether you have a buy-sell agreement, internal or external, and what it already commits you to
  • What happens to your clients and your family if you are unable to work before a plan is in place

You are the next generation

You have been promised equity. You do not know what kind, what it is worth, whether you can afford it, or what you are worth to the person offering it.

  • Whether it is equity at all, or a bonus plan wearing the word
  • What the buy-in costs against your actual income
  • The tax you may owe on money you never receive
  • What your staying is worth to the firm's sale price
