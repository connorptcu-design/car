About

I watched my father sell a practice he spent thirty-five years building.

Not as a consultant. As his son, from inside the family, through all the years of not deciding and then all of it at once.

Why this work

I saw which conversations kept getting postponed and what postponing them cost. I saw which questions turned out to matter and which ones everybody worried about for nothing. And I saw the parts that would have been worth a great deal more had anyone started three years earlier.

Before this I worked in asset management, with advisors across wirehouses, broker-dealers and independent firms, on how they run their practices rather than only what they invest in. I have sat with hundreds of teams. The same conversation is happening in almost all of them and almost none of them have anyone helping with it.

I am younger than most of the people I work with. That is worth saying plainly rather than hoping nobody notices. It also means I am closer in age to the successor than to the founder, and in a conversation that usually fails because those two cannot speak plainly to each other, that has turned out to be the useful seat in the room.

The work itself is not about wisdom. It is about arithmetic, sequencing, and asking questions in an order that makes them answerable. I build the models, I show the working, and you can check every assumption in them.

What you can expect Published fees. No success fee on any engagement. Every model open, with its assumptions visible and adjustable. And a direct answer when the honest one is that there is nothing here worth paying for yet.
Where we work Texas, Louisiana and Arkansas in person. Everywhere else by video, which is how most of this work happens anyway.
What we are not Not appraisers, not intermediaries, not a broker-dealer, and not your lawyer or your tax advisor. You need all of those at some point, and we will tell you when.
