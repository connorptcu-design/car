Why the models are open

A valuation whose assumptions you cannot see has to be taken on trust. Each model here shows its working, so you can test it. Change the recurring revenue share and watch the multiple move. Set growth to zero and see whether the conclusion still holds.

In the portal, the values you enter are saved to your own sign-in, so your scenarios are there next time on any device. Reset clears them, and you can also save a scenario file to your own computer before you raise it in a meeting.

Calibrated estimates, not appraisals Figures are illustrative and calibrated to publicly reported transaction norms. They are a starting point for a conversation, not a substitute for a formal valuation. When you need an appraisal for tax or regulatory purposes, we will tell you and help you find one.
