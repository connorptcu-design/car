Why they are open rather than locked

A valuation you cannot interrogate is a number you have to take on faith, and advisors are not in the business of taking numbers on faith. Every model here shows its working. Change the recurring revenue share and watch the multiple move. Set growth to zero and see whether the argument survives.

It also means you can run the scenario you were too polite to ask about in the meeting. Most of the useful conversations in this work start with a founder doing exactly that at eleven o'clock at night.

The models are calibrated, not authoritative Multiples and benchmark ranges come from publicly reported transaction norms, and they are a place to start a conversation rather than a substitute for a formal valuation. When you need an appraisal that will stand up for tax or regulatory purposes, we will tell you and help you find someone who does them.
